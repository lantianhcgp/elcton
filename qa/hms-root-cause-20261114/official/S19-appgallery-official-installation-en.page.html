      How to install AppGallery

      Step 1

      Click here to install AppGallery
      Install now

      Step 2

      Open AppGallery

      Step 3

      Search for "HMS Core" in AppGallery search bar and click "Install"

      Step 4

      Tap "Me" then choose "Log in to HUAWEI ID" to register your account

      Step 5

      Register HUAWEI ID using your phone number or email address

      Step 6

      Select your country of residence as per your HUAWEI ID registered country
      During the installation process, you may receive some standard warning messages generated by Android Operating System and not by AppGallery, which does not affect the flow or safety of the process.

      Step 7

      Now you’re all set to start exploring a world of apps and games and enjoy special offers and daily rewards with AppGallery
